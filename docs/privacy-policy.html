01 Overview & Core Purpose

Spend Tracer (package name com.spendguard) is a personal financial management and expense tracking mobile application for Android. The primary function of Spend Tracer is to help users maintain personal budget awareness by aggregating and categorizing daily debits, credits, and cash flows.

The application is designed to operate primarily offline. It does not require continuous internet access for expense tracking, analytics, or receipt logging.

02 Sensitive Permissions & Purpose

In accordance with Google Play's Permissions and APIs that Access Sensitive Information policy, Spend Tracer requests only the minimum set of permissions necessary to deliver core personal finance features:

Android Permission Usage & Core Functionality Handling Mode
RECEIVE_SMS Enables the application's background broadcast receiver (SmsReceiver) to detect incoming transactional SMS messages (credit and debit alerts from financial institutions) in real time. Local Only
READ_SMS Enables the user to import past financial messages when initiating an on-device SMS Sync or Rebuilding Transaction History. Local Only
INTERNET Required exclusively for initial authentication handshakes with Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication. No transaction data is transmitted over the internet. Auth Only
USE_BIOMETRIC Enables the native Android BiometricPrompt (fingerprint, face recognition, device PIN/pattern) for the in-app App Lock security gate. Local Hardware
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Enables local Android system notifications when a financial transaction has been successfully detected and parsed. Local Only

You may grant or revoke SMS permissions at any time through your Android device settings (Settings > Apps > Spend Tracer > Permissions). If SMS permissions are revoked, you can still manually record and track expenses.

03 Information Collected & How It Is Used

A. User Identity & Account Information:

  • Email Address and Display Name: Provided by your Google account when signing in via Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication. This information is used solely to authenticate your identity and personalize your greeting header.
  • Profile Picture URL: Displayed locally in your settings profile card.
  • User ID (UID): An alphanumeric identifier generated by Firebase to identify your local session.

B. Financial Transaction Information (Created or Parsed):

  • Transaction Amount: Numerical expenditure or income values.
  • Merchant / Beneficiary Name: The recipient or source of the transaction (e.g. UPI payee, store name).
  • Category: Financial categories such as Food, Groceries, Shopping, Friend, Cashback, Education, Transport.
  • Payment Mode: Payment channel (UPI, Debit Card, Credit Card, Net Banking, Cash).
  • Bank / Institution: Name of the bank issuing the alert (e.g. HDFC, SBI, ICICI, Axis).
  • Reference Number: Alphanumeric transaction IDs (UTR, RRN) parsed for deduplication.
  • Date and Timestamp: Exact time of transaction occurrence.

All financial transaction information is generated from your inputs or extracted from bank SMS messages, and is stored strictly within your device's sandboxed storage.

04 Financial SMS Reading & Local Processing

Spend Tracer implements strict safeguards when handling SMS messages on your device:

  • On-Device Heuristics & RegEx: The parsing pipeline uses regular expressions and on-device natural language classification rules to inspect text for banking identifiers, debit/credit indicators, and currency amounts.
  • Exclusion of Non-Financial Messages: Personal chat messages, OTPs (One-Time Passwords), verification codes, security codes, and marketing advertisements are explicitly rejected by the parsing engine and are never stored as transactions.
  • Zero Remote Transmission: SMS message text is never sent to external servers, cloud AI providers, or third-party analytical platforms. All processing occurs in the local execution context of your device.
  • User Control: You can turn SMS Monitoring ON or OFF at any time in Settings > AI & Automation > SMS Monitoring.

05 Data Storage: SQLite vs Cloud

We believe in total transparency regarding where and how data is persisted:

  • Local SQLite Database (Primary Storage): All transactions, incoming SMS logs, category rules, and spending analytics are stored in an on-device SQLite database named SpendGuardDB_v3.db located in the app's protected internal sandboxed directory (/data/data/com.spendguard/). No other application on your device can access this database.
  • Android Native SharedPreferences: Security settings, App Lock toggles, and lock timeout configurations are stored locally in SpendGuard_AppLock_v2.xml.
  • Cloud / Firestore Clarification: While the project repository contains Firebase configurations for Google authentication, the application does not sync, upload, or backup user transactions, balances, or SMS logs to Google Firestore or any remote cloud database. Your financial ledger remains entirely local to your device.

07 App Security & Biometric Lock

🔒 Built-in Hardware-Backed Privacy Protection

Spend Tracer incorporates multi-layered physical and software security controls to ensure your financial overview cannot be viewed by unauthorized individuals who have access to your unlocked phone.

  • Biometric App Lock: You can enable App Lock in Settings > Security & Privacy. When enabled, Spend Tracer requires biometric authentication (fingerprint or face) or device PIN/pattern to access any financial screen.
  • Window FLAG_SECURE: When App Lock is active, Spend Tracer automatically sets Android's FLAG_SECURE on its activity window. This prevents the operating system from saving readable thumbnails of your financial screens in the Android Recents / App Switcher overview and blocks third-party screen-capture utilities.
  • App Sandboxing: All data is stored within the Android internal sandbox protected by OS-level User ID isolation.

08 Data Export & Portability

Spend Tracer allows users to generate and export their financial ledger at any time in multiple formats:

  • PDF Statements: Branded, formatted expense summary reports generated entirely on-device via HTML-to-PDF compilation.
  • CSV / JSON Records: Raw transactional records for use in external spreadsheet or financial tools.

Exported documents are saved directly to your local device's Downloads or Documents folder. Spend Tracer does not transmit or store copies of your exported files on any external server.

09 Data Retention & Account Deletion

Spend Tracer is fully compliant with Google Play's Account Deletion Policy. Users have complete, autonomous control over both their authenticated cloud account and their on-device financial records:

🗑️ Dedicated Account Deletion Portal

If you have uninstalled Spend Tracer or wish to request permanent deletion of your account via the web, visit our dedicated portal:

👉 Spend Tracer Account & Data Deletion Portal (delete-account.html)

  • In-App Account Deletion (Instant): You can permanently delete your Firebase user account directly inside the app. Navigate to Settings > ACCOUNT ACTIONS > Delete Account. Confirming the destructive action will permanently erase your Firebase Authentication profile and UID, revoke Google Sign-In authorization tokens, and reset your session.
  • Delete All Transactions (Local Data): Kept strictly separate from Account Deletion, you can erase your entire on-device transaction ledger anytime by tapping Settings > DATA MANAGEMENT > Delete All Transactions.
  • Online Deletion Request (Uninstalled App): If you have uninstalled the app, you can submit a deletion request via our Account Deletion Portal or email us directly at ratheneshsg@gmail.com with the subject "Spend Tracer Account Deletion Request". Requests are processed within 7 business days.
  • Data Deleted: All cloud authentication profiles, Firebase UID, registered email address, display name, and avatar tokens are permanently destroyed.
  • Legally Retained Data: None. Spend Tracer does not operate as a financial institution, credit broker, or payment processor, and retains zero user data for statutory or regulatory compliance after deletion.
  • Complete On-Device Data Purge: All transactions are stored locally on your device in SQLite (SpendGuardDB_v3.db). You can purge this at any time by clearing app data in Android Settings > Apps > Spend Tracer > Storage & Cache > Clear Storage or simply uninstalling the application.

10 User Rights & Choices

Under applicable global data privacy regulations (including GDPR, CCPA, and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act), you have the following rights regarding your information:

  • Right to Access: You can view all transactions and extracted financial information directly in the app dashboard, transaction list, and analytics views.
  • Right to Rectification: You can edit any transaction's amount, category, merchant name, or payment mode at any time.
  • Right to Erasure: You can erase your entire history using the data clearing options described in Section 9.
  • Right to Portability: You can export your data to open formats (CSV/JSON/PDF) whenever you wish.
  • Right to Revoke Consent: You can toggle off SMS monitoring or revoke Android SMS permissions at any time without terminating app functionality.
