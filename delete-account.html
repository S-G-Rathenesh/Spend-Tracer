Account & Data Deletion Request

In accordance with Google Play's Account Deletion Policy, Spend Tracer allows any user to permanently delete their account and associated data at any time — either directly within the application or via this public webpage if the app has been uninstalled.

Application: Spend Tracer (com.spendguard)
Developer: S G Rathenesh
Dedicated Contact: ratheneshsg@gmail.com

Method 1 In-App Account Deletion (Installed App)

If you currently have Spend Tracer installed on your Android device, you can delete your account instantly with zero waiting time:

  1. Open the Spend Tracer app on your Android device.
  2. Tap the Settings tab in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Scroll down to the ACCOUNT ACTIONS section.
  4. Tap Delete Account (marked in red).
  5. A confirmation screen will outline the permanent deletion details. Tap Permanently Delete Account.
  6. Your Firebase user profile and authentication credentials will be immediately destroyed, and you will be signed out.
Note on Local Transaction Data: Because Spend Tracer stores transaction records locally on your device using SQLite, deleting your account revokes your cloud identity. To erase local transactions from your device as well, tap Delete All Transactions in Settings, or clear app storage via Android Settings.

Method 2 Online Deletion Request (If App is Uninstalled)

If you have uninstalled Spend Tracer or no longer have access to your Android device, you can submit a deletion request directly to our developer team. We will delete your account from Firebase within 7 business days.

Data Deletion & Retention Breakdown

We are committed to total transparency regarding what data is deleted upon request and our retention policies:

Data Category Specific Data Elements Deletion Action Retention Policy
Account Credentials Google Sign-In UID, Email Address, Display Name, Profile Photo URL. Permanently Deleted from Firebase Authentication. Zero retention after deletion.
Cloud Financial Data Transactions, SMS logs, account balances. N/A (Never uploaded to the cloud; Spend Tracer does not maintain a remote cloud database for transactions). Zero cloud retention at all times.
Local Device Data SQLite transaction database (SpendGuardDB_v3.db), SMS classification rules. Deleted locally when the user taps Delete All Transactions, clears app storage, or uninstalls the app. Controlled 100% by the user on device.
Legally Retained Data None. Spend Tracer is not a licensed financial institution, payment gateway, or credit broker. We do not retain any user data for statutory or regulatory compliance after deletion. No data retained.
